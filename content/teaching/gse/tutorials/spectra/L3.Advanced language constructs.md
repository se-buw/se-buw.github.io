In this module, you work with pattern libraries and past-time operators.

## What you learn

- How to import and instantiate reusable temporal patterns.
- How to express history-dependent requirements with past operators.
- How patterns and past operators affect synthesis behavior.

Patterns provide reusable temporal templates. In this module, they come from the Dwyer pattern catalog and must be imported before use.

When you instantiate a pattern as an assumption or guarantee, do not prefix it with `ini`, `alw`, or `alwEv`; the temporal structure is inside the pattern itself.

Past operators such as `ONCE`, `HISTORICALLY`, and `PREV` let you express constraints that refer to history, including nested past expressions.

## How to work through this page

1. Inspect the traffic model that imports the pattern library.
2. Locate one pattern instantiation and map it to its intent.
3. Add the task assumption and recheck realizability.
