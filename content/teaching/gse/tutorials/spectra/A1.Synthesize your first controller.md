In this module, you check realizability and synthesize a controller if a specification is realizable.

## What you learn

- How to distinguish fast realizability checking from full controller synthesis.
- How to read concrete controller strategies at a high level.
- Why seemingly small changes to liveness formulas can change realizability.

## How to work through this page

1. Read the base specification and predict whether it is realizable.
2. Run realizability and synthesis in Eclipse.
3. Compare `TrafficA1a.spectra` and `TrafficA1b.spectra` and explain the difference.

To inspect a synthesized strategy in Eclipse, right-click in the editor and use `Spectra -> Synthesize Concrete Controller`.

For larger models, prefer `Check Realizability` first. If realizable, use a symbolic controller for further simulation and execution, because concrete transition systems can become too large to inspect.
