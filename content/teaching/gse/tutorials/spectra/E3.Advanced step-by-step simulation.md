In this module, you use advanced controller-walker features such as breakpoints and reachability exploration.

## What you learn

- How to formulate useful breakpoint expressions.
- How to check and witness reachability.
- How reachability traces reveal hidden modeling issues.

Recommended workflow:

1. Synthesize a static symbolic controller.
2. Open Rich Controller Walker.
3. Add breakpoint expressions (for example, `p1`, `p2`, `p3`, or custom coordinate predicates).
4. Run `Check Reachability` for a breakpoint and switch to reachability mode to follow a witness trace.
