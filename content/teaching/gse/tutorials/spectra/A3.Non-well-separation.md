In this module, you inspect non-well-separation and compare repair strategies.

## What you learn

- What non-well-separation means semantically.
- Why a realizable specification can still be problematic.
- How to diagnose and repair environment assumptions.

## How to work through this page

1. Check realizability first.
2. Run well-separation diagnosis.
3. Compute a non-well-separation core.
4. Propose either assumption-side or guarantee-side repairs.

Non-well-separation means that from a reachable state, the system can force the environment to violate an assumption. This is often undesired even when the specification is realizable.

Use `Spectra Add-ons -> Well-Separation` to diagnose the issue and compute a non-well-separation core.
