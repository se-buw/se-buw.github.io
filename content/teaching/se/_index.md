**This site presents a collection of practice exams created for to the Software Engineering course at [Bauhaus University Weimar.](https://www.uni-weimar.de)**

Most practice exams come in English and German, but some are only available in English. The exams are provided as PDF files. For many exams we provide a marking guide, which is also available as PDF file. The marking guides are provided in English only. 

We suggest that you first try to solve the exam on your own and then use the marking guide to check your solution. Note that the marking guide may in some cases contain a solution, but in general it only contains the marking scheme. For one or two questions we might not have correctly updated the marking guide. In these cases it may refer to a similar question from a previous exam, so please check the marking guide carefully.
